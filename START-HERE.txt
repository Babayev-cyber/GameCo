OCUUPATION OF MARS — 3D multiplayer prototype

Bu qovluğun İÇİNDƏKİ faylları ayrıca GitHub reposunun kökünə yüklə.
Digər çatın faylları ilə qarışmaması üçün versiya mars-3d içindədir.

LOKAL İŞƏ SALMA
1. Node.js 24 quraşdır.
2. Bu qovluqda terminal aç:
   npm install
   npm start
3. http://localhost:3001 ünvanına daxil ol.
4. Create a room seç. İkinci brauzer pəncərəsində həmin kodla qoşul.
   Eyni Wi-Fi-da telefon üçün kompüterin lokal IP-si ilə :3001 aç.
   Windows firewall soruşsa yalnız özəl şəbəkə üçün Node-a icazə ver.

GITHUB PAGES + MULTIPLAYER
GitHub Pages yalnız statik faylları yayımlayır. Multiplayer Node serveri
ayrıca işləməlidir. Server bütün oyunu da təkbaşına yayımlaya bilər.

1. Qovluğun məzmununu (.github daxil) GitHub reposunun main branch-inə yüklə.
2. Render-də həmin repodan Node Web Service yarat (render.yaml hazırdır).
   Build command: npm ci
   Start command: npm start
   Health check: /health
   Serverin HTTPS ünvanını götür.
3. public/config.js içində GAME_SERVER_URL dəyərini dəyiş:
   window.GAME_SERVER_URL = 'wss://SENIN-SERVERIN.onrender.com';
4. GitHub > Settings > Pages > Source: GitHub Actions seç.
5. Dəyişikliyi main-ə push et. Publish Mars 3D workflow saytı yayımlayacaq.
   Link adətən https://USERNAME.github.io/REPO/ formasındadır.

1v1 TEST FLIGHT
Gəmini seç və TEST FLIGHT düyməsinə bas. 16 saniyə yüklənmə və 5 saniyə geri sayımdan sonra KEPLER botu ilə 1v1 başlayır.
Bot hərəkət edir, nişan alır və fasilələrlə atəş açır. Məhv olduqda 4 saniyəyə qayıdır.
RESTART TEST xal, sağlamlıq və mövqeləri sıfırlayır. Botun nişanı onun yerini göstərir.
Mars səth xəritəsinin mənbəsi və krediti: public/assets/CREDITS.txt.

WEAPONS: Hold LEFT CLICK or SPACE for lasers. Press R or RIGHT CLICK for a guided rocket (6-second cooldown). Touch controls have LASER and ROCKET buttons. Keep the aim on an enemy within 1500 m for 0.65 seconds; launch when TARGET LOCKED appears. The large orbit rings have been removed; capture distance remains in the HUD.

GAME OVER: Human pilots no longer respawn automatically. TRY AGAIN restarts a practice match. In multiplayer, REDEPLOY becomes available after four seconds. Bots still respawn automatically.
Visual update: removed the foreground moons and beacon, added three detailed procedural ship models, deployable rocket fins, rocket acceleration and trails, muzzle flashes and larger explosions.

REALISM AND PERFORMANCE UPDATE
Mars uses the NASA photographic surface, restrained daylight and a thinner atmosphere.
Exterior ships have beveled hulls, panel seams, metal reflections and throttle-dependent exhaust.
The uploaded cockpit has working speed, heading, radar, hull and weapon displays.
The cockpit shell stays imported; decorative paper, markers and floating glyphs are hidden at runtime.
Drive vibration and rocket launch audio respect SOUND OFF and pause when the page is hidden.
Acceleration and braking are gradual. Remote ships interpolate between network snapshots;
the local view briefly predicts movement, including smooth yaw and pitch.
Rendering resolution adjusts to slow frames. Covered menu rendering pauses, the hangar
renders at up to 30 FPS, ship buffers are shared, and lasers/particles use bounded pools.
The five-minute timer uses wall-clock time. Missile steering is independent of tick duration.
See PERFORMANCE.txt for measurements and their limits. A fixed FPS on every device or
connection cannot be guaranteed; no physical-phone performance claim is made.

UPLOADED GLB ASSETS
The four user-provided GLBs are bundled under public/assets/models/. The cockpit is used for the pilot view; the two missile GLBs are used by opposing teams; sparksexplosion.glb augments impacts. Models load once and share geometry. Camera placement and some overly bright decorative cockpit layers are adjusted at runtime. Original GLB files are unchanged.
The flight deck shows the same procedural exterior models used in combat, with drag-to-rotate preview and a separate imported COCKPIT preview. No exterior spacecraft GLB was present among the uploads.


FLIGHT CONTROLS UPDATE
Opposing fleets start 3500 m apart. Mouse moves the weapon reticle across the forward view; A/D and arrow keys still steer the spacecraft. Shift alone accelerates forward at 2.4x cruise speed. Edge streaks, wider field of view, and the speed readout indicate boost. W/S control normal forward/reverse thrust. R/right-click only launches after a server-confirmed target lock. Moving aim off target loses the lock before firing; an already launched missile retains its target.
4v4 and 8v8 require 8 or 16 connected human players; the host cannot bypass this check. No bots fill multiplayer slots. Only TEST FLIGHT contains KEPLER.

LAUNCH, SKY AND AUDIO UPDATE
Every launch, including TEST FLIGHT, RESTART TEST and rematches, runs loading.mp4
for 16 seconds, then a synchronized five-second countdown and START.
Startingspeech.mp3 plays from launch through the countdown. SOUND OFF mutes it.
No pilot can move or shoot before START. If assets are still downloading after
16 seconds, loading waits for every human pilot to be ready. A missing pilot
cancels multiplayer loading/countdown and returns the remaining pilots to the lobby.

Hull integrity: INTERCEPTOR 170 HP, VANGUARD 240 HP, SENTINEL 320 HP.
The 3500 m separation gives even two interceptors boosting toward each other
more than five seconds before they can enter the 900 m laser range.
Rockets require a 0.65-second aim lock within 1500 m, retain that target in flight,
accelerate from the launch rail and have an eight-second lifetime.

Laser shots use lasergun.mp3. Missile detonations use explosion.mp3.
An enemy missile targeting your pilot triggers the visible MISSILE INBOUND alert
and repeats only the first three seconds of warning.mp3 until the threat is gone.
Mute, leaving the match and hiding the tab stop the warning sound.
The cockpit eye position and field of view now leave more of the canopy open.
Stars, a faint galactic band, Earth and Jupiter use a small number of extra draws;
Earth/Jupiter textures and credits are included in public/assets/CREDITS.txt.
Red laser bolts have an illuminated core, travelling pulse, corona, impact flare
and brief hull lighting. Bloom runs during laser effects on suitable desktop
resolution; phones and reduced resolution use the direct renderer.
The loading cinematic is a 1280 x 720 H.264 MP4 (about 2.4 MB), reduced from the
29 MB 4K upload. The original upload is unchanged. Initial downloads depend
on the connection; browsers cache the smaller copy for later launches.

PART II: MARS SURFACE
Destroy every ship in the opposing fleet at the same time to unlock landing.
The server checks this condition, stops orbital combat and starts a 6.5-second
descent. The winning human team deploys together with 120 suit health each.
The losing team spectates. A bot victory in solo flight leaves GAME OVER visible.
The original orbital capture/time limit still ends a match; a full fleet defeat
is the route into the second mission.

Clear three waves of Martian creatures. Scouts have 60 HP, stalkers 144 HP,
and brutes 336 HP, with different sizes, movement speeds and attack damage.
Waves scale with squad size. Monsters approach around rocks and attack nearby
soldiers. The server controls movement, shots, creature health and wave progress.
Boulders and terrain block laser shots. A squad wipe or the eight-minute surface
limit fails the mission. Living squad members can fight on while a fallen teammate
waits four seconds to redeploy; if everyone dies, the operation must be replayed.

The first-person rifle is the uploaded gunlowpoly.glb, bundled unchanged, with
procedural spacesuit sleeves and gloves. Its meshes are merged at runtime for
one rifle draw. It fires the same red laser shader and lasergun.mp3 as the ships.
WASD: walk/strafe. SHIFT: sprint. LEFT CLICK / SPACE: fire. Hold RIGHT CLICK:
aim down the sights with a narrower field of view and lower mouse sensitivity.
Click the view for continuous mouse look; ESC releases it. Arrow keys can also
turn/look. Phone controls: left stick, drag the view to look, LASER, AIM, SPRINT.

TEST MARS SURFACE on the flight deck opens a clearly marked solo surface test.
It uses the normal 16-second loading and five-second countdown, then descent,
so the second part can be tested without winning another space battle.
All online 4v4/8v8 rooms still require real humans and earn landing through combat.
Terrain, rocky ridges, dust, landed ships and creature models use local low poly
geometry. Rocks and ridges use instancing; the game reuses its existing renderer.
Surface shaders prepare while the launch assets load.
Browser check: node scripts/check-surface-browser.mjs (Chrome CDP on port 9331).
