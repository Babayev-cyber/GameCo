User-provided media: background.mp3, marswalk.mp4, Startingspeech.mp3,
warning.mp3, explosion.mp3, lasergun.mp3 and loading.mp4.
The loading cinematic is resized from 4K to 1280 x 720 and encoded to H.264 MP4
at a target 1.4 Mbps using Chrome MediaRecorder. Original upload is unchanged.
Other user audio/video files are bundled unchanged; warning.mp3 playback uses
only its first three seconds, copied into an AudioBuffer at runtime.

User-provided gunlowpoly.glb is bundled unchanged under assets/models/.
Its geometry is merged, normalized and placed in the soldier view at runtime.
Spacesuit hands, surface terrain, rocks, ridges and creature geometry are procedural.
